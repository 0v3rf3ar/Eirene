# Company newsletter

## What it is

A newsletter summarising the past week or month for the whole organisation,
roughly 20–25 bullets. It goes out by chat and email, so it has to read well in
both.

It should have:

- **Many links:** the documents that matter, notable announcement posts and
  leadership messages, organisation-wide emails, and anything significant that
  happened.
- **Short bullets:** one or two sentences each.
- **"We" voice:** you are part of the organisation — "we shipped", "we hired",
  "we opened".

## Sources

Use what you can reach; if you cannot reach something useful, tell the person the
newsletter would be better with it.

- chat: posts in large channels with many reactions or long threads;
- email: organisation-wide announcements from leadership;
- calendar: meetings with large attendee lists, such as all-hands or major
  announcements, and the documents attached to them;
- documents: widely read new docs from the last week or two — vision documents,
  quarterly or half-year plans, documents by senior leaders;
- press: articles or coverage the organisation received.

With no access, ask the person what to include and focus on shaping their
material into this format.

## Sections

Assume a large organisation (1,000+ people) with many teams and initiatives.
Group related items into sections, for example {product, go-to-market, finance},
{hiring, execution, vision} or {external news, internal news}, so every major
area shows up.

## What to include

Prefer:

- organisation-wide impact over team detail;
- announcements from leadership;
- major milestones and achievements;
- information that affects most people;
- external recognition and press.

Leave out:

- fine-grained team updates (those belong in 3Ps);
- items that matter to only a small group;
- things already communicated elsewhere.
