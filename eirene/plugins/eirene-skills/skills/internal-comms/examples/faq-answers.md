# FAQ round-up

## What it is

Each week many questions get asked across an organisation. Your job is to find
the ones causing the most confusion for the most people and give each a short,
reliable answer, so everyone stays informed and aligned. Two tasks only:

- find questions that confuse many people, usually about things that affect a
  large part of the organisation;
- answer each one briefly to clear up the confusion.

Typical topics: recent events (funding, new leaders), upcoming launches, hiring,
changes in strategy or focus.

## Sources

Look where communication and work happen:

- chat: questions in replies to busy posts, questions with many reactions or
  "+1"s, anything showing many people want the same answer;
- email: messages that already contain FAQs;
- documents: FAQs written into docs, or questions you can infer from a doc linked
  on an important calendar event.

## Format

Keep it simple:

```
- *Question*: [one sentence]
- *Answer*: [one or two sentences]
```

## Coverage

Cover the whole organisation, not just the requester's team. Read as widely as
the available sources allow and pick questions that matter to everyone.

## Answers

- Base answers on official communication wherever possible.
- Say clearly when something is uncertain.
- Link the authoritative source (doc, announcement, email).
- Professional but approachable.
- Flag questions that need an answer from leadership.
