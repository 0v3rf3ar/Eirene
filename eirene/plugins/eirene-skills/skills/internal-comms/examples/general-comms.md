# Other internal communication

## What it is

Internal writing that is not a 3P update, a newsletter or an FAQ — for example a
status report, a project update, an incident report, or an announcement.

Before writing:

1. Ask who the audience is.
2. Understand what the message is for.
3. Confirm the tone: formal, casual, urgent or informational.
4. Check for any required format or template.

## Principles

- Clear and short.
- Active voice.
- Most important information first.
- Link the relevant references.
- Match how the organisation usually writes.

## Common shapes

**Status report** — current state in one line (on track / at risk / off track),
then what changed since last time, what is next, and what needs a decision.

**Project update** — goal, progress against milestones with dates, risks, and
asks.

**Incident report** — summary and impact (who was affected, for how long), a
timeline with times and time zone, root cause, what fixed it, and follow-up
actions with owners. Keep it blameless: describe systems and decisions, not
people's faults.
