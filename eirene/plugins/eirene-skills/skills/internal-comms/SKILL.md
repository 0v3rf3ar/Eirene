# Internal communications

## Use this for

- 3P updates (Progress, Plans, Problems)
- company or organisation newsletters
- FAQ round-ups and answers
- status reports and leadership updates
- project updates
- incident reports

## How to write one

1. **Work out the type** of communication from the request.
2. **Read the matching guide** in `examples/`:
   - `examples/3p-updates.md` — weekly Progress / Plans / Problems updates
   - `examples/company-newsletter.md` — organisation-wide newsletters
   - `examples/faq-answers.md` — collecting and answering common questions
   - `examples/general-comms.md` — anything that does not fit the others,
     including status reports, project updates and incident reports
3. **Follow that guide** for format, tone and how to gather the content.

If the request does not match any guide, ask what format and audience the person
wants before drafting.

## Gathering content

Use whatever sources this session can reach — chat or email connectors, document
stores, calendars, issue trackers, the repository's history — and say which ones
you used. When you cannot reach a source, ask the person to paste or summarise
what should be covered. Never invent metrics, names, dates or quotes; mark gaps
with `[TBD: …]` instead.
