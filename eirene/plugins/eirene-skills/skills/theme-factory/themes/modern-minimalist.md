# Modern Minimalist

Clean and current, a restrained greyscale that suits almost anything.

## Colors

- **Charcoal** `#36454f` — primary dark
- **Slate Gray** `#708090` — mid-grey accent
- **Light Gray** `#d3d3d3` — backgrounds and dividers
- **White** `#ffffff` — text and clean backgrounds

## Fonts

- **Headers:** DejaVu Sans Bold
- **Body:** DejaVu Sans

## Fits

Tech talks, architecture portfolios, design showcases, business proposals, data visualisation.
